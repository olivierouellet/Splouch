{# The manual console — event and heat by hand, for a meet with no timing console. Phone-first: the operator is standing on the pool deck holding this, not sitting at the admin laptop. Hence the fixed header showing what is actually on the boards, a pair of thumb-sized Prev/Next buttons under it, and the meet's whole running order below as a scrollable list. Two ways to change the heat, both deliberate: - Prev / Next, held for 1.5 s (the [data-hold] mechanism the Power tab's Reboot button uses), for the ordinary step between heats. - Tap a row to preview its swimmers, then tap that row's ▸ to commit it, for jumping somewhere else in the meet. A plain tap on a row never changes the board. Changing the heat mid-race is the mistake this page has to be hard to make by accident. The whole start list is embedded, not fetched: previewing then costs nothing, and the page survives a phone that drops off the Wi-Fi between heats. Seed times are deliberately left out of the preview — this page is about who is in the water, and there are no times here to compare them against. #} {{ t.title or 'Manual console' }}
