{# The live board on a phone — one page, served by both servers. The Pi renders it for operators checking the pool setup before a meet and watching times land as fast as the console reports them. The cloud renders the same page as a fallback for spectators when the native apps are unavailable. Neither audience wants chrome: there is no carousel, no test banner, no column animation and no timed hold on a state. What is on screen is the last frame received, which is what makes it useful to an operator and safe for a phone that reconnects mid-meet.
